Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

What does this calculator include?

It calculates your real profit after platform fees, payment processing fees, and product costs. See exactly what you keep.

How are platform fees calculated?

Each platform charges a percentage of revenue plus fixed fees per sale. We calculate both to show your total platform cost.

Can I edit the fees?

Yes. Click "Edit Fees" to adjust any platform's fee structure or create a custom configuration.

Does this calculator save my data?

No. All calculations are done locally in your browser. We don't store, track, or collect any of your information.

Which platforms are supported?

We include presets for Gumroad, Payhip, and Etsy. You can also create a completely custom configuration.

Is this calculator free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up required. Use it as many times as you want.
