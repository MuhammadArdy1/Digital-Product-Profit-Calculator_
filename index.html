Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Clear context for planning your next digital product.

What is a digital product profit calculator?

A planning tool that helps estimate revenue, costs, fees and profit for products delivered digitally.

What fees should I include?

Include platform, payment processing and fixed transaction fees.

Can I use this calculator for Gumroad?

Yes. Select Gumroad, then review the editable fee fields to match your rates.

Can I use this calculator for Payhip?

Yes. Select Payhip and adjust the editable fee fields when needed.

Can I use this calculator for Etsy?

Yes. Select Etsy, remembering that rates and fees may vary by category.

Does the calculator store my data?

This foundation is designed to run locally in your browser. No data is stored or sent anywhere.
